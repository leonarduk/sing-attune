Add Issue Templates for Better Quality and Consistency

## What

Introduce structured issue templates to enhance the quality and consistency of issues filed on this repository.

## Why

Improving the structure of issue reports will make it easier for maintainers and contributors to understand the context, reproduce the problem, and provide effective solutions. This will reduce response times and ensure that all necessary information is captured in each issue.

## How

- Copy existing issue templates from other reputable repositories
- Ensure the templates include sections such as 'What', 'Why', 'How', 'Constraints', 'LLM tier', 'Success looks like', and 'Failure looks like'
- Customize the templates to fit the specific needs of this repository

## Files Affected

.github

## Constraints

None

## LLM tier

sonnet

## Value

Medium Value

## Success looks like

- [ ] Structured issue templates are added to the .github directory
- [ ] The new templates include all necessary sections as specified
- [ ] Existing issues are reviewed and updated to conform to the new template format if applicable

## Failure looks like

- [ ] No changes made to issue templates or existing issues
